World news in one image — Sunday, 22 November 2026.

A make.fun entry for the bounty
[news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122):
one image that sums up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026.

The image is a newspaper-style front page led by a full-width hero
story — COP31 collapses with no deal in Antalya — above seven more
photo stories from that day: three tankers hit near the Strait of
Hormuz, a Russian drone hits a block of flats near Kyiv, BepiColombo
reaches Mercury after eight years, Marc Marquez seals his eighth
MotoGP title in Portugal, the Democrats take 258 House seats, the
FBI crime database is hacked, and a funeral boat sinks in Nigeria —
plus a ticker with GTA VI making $3.79 billion in a day, Bitcoin
falling below $57,000, and West Bank settlers torching vehicles.
Headlines and summaries use large type and every summary is at most
12 words, so the page still reads at small sizes. The photos are
AI-generated illustrations (no real press photos, no identifiable
people); all text is rendered with Python PIL and DejaVu fonts, and
every fact is verified.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so both runs write identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```
